## Classification

- All 6 groups: **UNRESOLVED_DUPLICATE**, sub-class SAME_CONTENT_SAME_ATTACK_UNKNOWN_IDENTITY.
- Why not KNOWN_REDUNDANT_COPY: no source states that these are packaging copies.
- Why not SAME_CONTENT_SAME_IDENTITY: identity is unknown (Q-14), even though identical bytes imply the same recorded person.

## Findings

- **FACT:** the official Protocol I lists place identical content in **both** train and test for 3 pairs (4, 5, 6). The official protocol itself would leak identical videos across its own train/test.
- **FACT:** all duplicates are within `Spoof/Replay` (98 videos → 92 distinct contents).
- **Requirement for M3 (any SiW split, if ever unblocked):** identical-content groups must be assigned to one split as a unit, keyed by sha256. The official list membership must not be reused as a split.
