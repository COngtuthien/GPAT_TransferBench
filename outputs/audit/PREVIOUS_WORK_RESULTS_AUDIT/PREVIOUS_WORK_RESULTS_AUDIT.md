## 3. E04 pre-launch logging: `E04_PRELAUNCH_LOGGING_READY`

Sources: `methods/physics_std/production_runner.py`, `production_io.py`, `losses.py`, and the D12 requalification records (`E04_PRODUCTION_QUALIFICATION_D12.json`; host `qualification/e04_production/E04/*-0f86725250f57541/metrics.jsonl`).

The production loop writes one fsync'd record per iteration (150 000 records) containing:
- **Losses:** step-1 `loss_G_step1, L_depth, L_G, L_P, L_R, P_positive, P0_negative_prior`; step-2 `loss_D, L_D`; step-3 `loss_G_step3, L_S, L_H`.
- **Learning rates:** `learning_rates {G, D}`, asserted against the schedule every iteration.
- **Counters:** `global_step`, asserted to be t+1 at phase 0.
- **Data and RNG:** pair rows, ids and passes; hard-sample draws; input SHA256 values.
- **Timing and memory:** `step_seconds`, `wall_clock_seconds`, `utc`, and GPU MaxBytesInUse.

There are 10 checkpoint events: every 15 000 iterations, with UTC, state SHA256, round-trip and finiteness checks. Iteration 150000 is BASELINE_FINAL_STATE_V1. Identities cover the config, spec, A4, M6D3d, rulings, locks, pairs, split, pre-pass, feed pack, worker graph and production files. `run_start` records the driver (D9).

Items that need only post-processing, with no runner change:
- **Optimizer applications.** These are exact as G = 2·global_step and D = global_step: every logged iteration executed `apply[1..3]`, or the run raised.
- **D12 disclosure.** The D12 amendment hash is not embedded in `identities()`. It is recoverable through `run_manifest.git_commit`, because the launch commit contains the OWNER_APPROVED D12 yaml. The seed completion audit must cite it.

Not recorded, and not required by `run_logging_v1` or the frozen spec:
- Gradient norms. Adding gradient fetches would change the executed subgraph and need requalification.
- A per-discriminator split of L_G/L_D.
- GPU co-tenant load.

The co-tenant load can be captured without touching the runner, by an optional external `nvidia-smi` sampler. At audit time another user's job was using 95% of the GPU and about 5.2 GB.

Storage: about 2.4 KB per record, so about 0.36 GB of metrics plus about 10 × 24 MB of checkpoints per seed. The host has 89 GB free (90% used).

Timing: the original qualification measured 0.944 / 0.942 s/iteration, about 39 h per seed (`E04_PRODUCTION_QUALIFICATION.md`). The D12 requalification's 3.10–3.20 s/iteration was measured under concurrent CPU pre-pass load, and its notes disclose it as not representative (`E04_PRODUCTION_QUALIFICATION_D12_NOTES.md`). A co-tenant GPU job would slow the run further.

**No gap makes a required paper result irrecoverable, so E04 seed 42 can be launched without losing essential publication data.** Launching still needs its own owner decision. Checklist: `E_e04_prelaunch_logging_checklist.csv`.
