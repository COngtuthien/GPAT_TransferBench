[Heading1] 1. Research method and hypotheses
A sample for source-conditioned transfer contains a live target x_t and a real spoof source x_s. A generator G produces a synthetic spoof x_hat whose facial content should remain attributable to x_t while its attack evidence should be attributable to x_s.
x_hat = G(x_t, x_s; supervision, method parameters)
[ListBullet] H1 — Target preservation: GPAT has higher target identity similarity and lower geometric/topological drift than unconstrained full-image conversion methods.
[ListBullet] H2 — Artifact transfer: GPAT does not “win” by producing an almost unchanged live face; source-to-synthetic artifact similarity and attack-family consistency must remain non-collapsed.
[ListBullet] H3 — Downstream utility: replacing a fixed portion of real-spoof training examples with an equal-budget GPAT synthetic bank improves or maintains real held-out FAS performance relative to other generators.
[ListBullet] H4 — Supervision effect: attack-type labels improve artifact controllability; identity supervision primarily reduces source-identity leakage. These effects are tested separately with GPAT B0/B1/B2/B3.
[ListBullet] H5 — Hard wavelet constraint: γ=0 and wavelet residual output produce a better geometry–artifact Pareto trade-off than full RGB generation, RGB residual generation, or γ>0 variants.
[ListBullet] H6 — Improvements are not explained by more synthetic data, a stronger evaluator, a different threshold, generator fingerprint, or data leakage.
[Heading2] 1.1 Primary contribution claim allowed after experiments
The paper may claim a “geometry-preserving, source-conditioned, bounded wavelet-residual spoof transfer framework” only if H1, H2 and H5 are supported. The broad statements “first spoof transfer”, “first frequency-domain FAS”, “first spoof map” and “first quality-aware FAS” are prohibited because related prior work exists.

[Heading2] 3.4 Frame sampling from videos / image sequences
[ListBullet] Sample exactly 8 unique frames per canonical video/sequence whenever at least 8 valid frames exist.
[ListBullet] Normalized sample positions are fixed at [0.10, 0.2142857, 0.3285714, 0.4428571, 0.5571429, 0.6714286, 0.7857143, 0.90] of the valid frame range; choose the nearest unique valid frame indices.
[ListBullet] For sequences shorter than 8 valid frames, use every unique valid frame once; never duplicate a frame to reach eight.
[ListBullet] Decode with the same backend and record decoder/version. Extract canonical PNGs losslessly before downstream preprocessing.
[ListBullet] No temporal model is used in the core benchmark; the eight frames are independent training samples. Video-level test score is the arithmetic mean of the eight frame probabilities.
[Heading2] 3.5 Frozen split algorithm — 70/15/15 subject-disjoint
[ListBullet] Split independently inside each dataset, then pool the dataset-specific TRAIN/VAL/TEST partitions.
[ListBullet] Grouping key = subject_id_global. A subject must appear in exactly one split.
[ListBullet] Target ratios by number of canonical videos are TRAIN 0.70, VAL 0.15, TEST 0.15.
[ListBullet] Deterministic split_seed = 20260814.
[ListBullet] Allocator objective: minimize absolute deviation from target video counts plus penalties for missing binary class and attack_macro coverage. If an attack macro has fewer than 10 unique subjects, coverage across all three splits is not forced; this limitation is reported.
[ListBullet] No video fallback is allowed in the main protocol. If a dataset adapter cannot recover subject ID, the split milestone is BLOCKED_BY_MISSING_SUBJECT_ID. A video-disjoint fallback may be implemented only as a separately named protocol_v1_video_fallback, never as the main result.

[Heading1] 7. Synthetic budget and two fairness views
[ListBullet] Main intended budget: N_syn = N_train_spoof_frames.
[ListBullet] Every synthetic output must be returned as canonical 256×256 RGB uint8, regardless of the method’s internal resolution.
[ListBullet] RAW-MATCHED view: retain every numerically valid generated output; only NaN/Inf, decode failure or missing output is excluded. This view isolates the raw generator.
[ListBullet] QUALITY-MATCHED view: each method may apply its native documented filtering/quality mechanism. Determine N_common = minimum number of valid/accepted outputs among compared methods; deterministically subsample every method to N_common using pair_id/synthetic_id hash. This prevents GPAT quality filtering from receiving a larger or smaller training budget.
[ListBullet] Report both RAW-MATCHED and QUALITY-MATCHED downstream results. The paper must state which one is the primary table before inspecting TEST.

[Heading2] 8.2 Frequency Substitution — controlled implementation of Huang et al. synthesis idea
[ListBullet] Status: CONTROLLED_ADAPTATION because the paper describes random high-frequency block substitution but does not fully fix every block geometry/implementation detail needed for a deterministic modern implementation.
[ListBullet] Compute per-channel FFT2 of target and source at 256×256. Let A_t, φ_t and A_s be target magnitude, target phase and source magnitude after fftshift.
[ListBullet] Eligible high-frequency region: radial normalized frequency r ∈ [0.20, 0.50] where r=0 at spectrum center and r=0.5 at Nyquist edge.
[ListBullet] Partition the shifted magnitude spectrum into non-overlapping 16×16 blocks. Eligible block if at least 75% of its pixels lie in the eligible high-frequency region.
[ListBullet] Select exactly 25% of eligible blocks using pair-specific RNG seed. Replace target magnitude with source magnitude in selected blocks and the conjugate-symmetric counterparts. Preserve target phase.
[ListBullet] Reconstruct complex spectrum A_mix·exp(jφ_t), inverse shift, inverse FFT, real part, clip to [0,255]. No spatial post-filter.
[ListBullet] Binary and Native tracks are identical. Save block mask as the method’s edit map for diagnostics.

[Heading1] 9. GPAT exact architecture — frozen proposed method
Figure 4. Frozen GPAT generator architecture for this benchmark.
[Heading2] 9.1 Input/output contract
z_a = E_art(x_s)
DWT(x_t) = {LL_t, LH_t, HL_t, HH_t}
{ΔLL, ΔLH, ΔHL, ΔHH, M} = G_res(DWT(x_t), z_a)
x_hat = IDWT(LL_t + γΔLL, LH_t + M⊙ΔLH, HL_t + M⊙ΔHL, HH_t + M⊙ΔHH)
Main configuration: γ=0.0. Therefore the generator has no path to alter the target LL band in the primary GPAT result. γ ∈ {0.02, 0.05, 0.10} is an ablation only.
[Heading2] 9.2 Artifact Encoder E_art — exact implementation
[ListBullet] Backbone: torchvision ResNet-18 with IMAGENET1K_V1 initialization.
[ListBullet] Input tensor size: 12×256×256. Channels = 3 high-pass RGB channels + upsampled source detail bands [LH_s, HL_s, HH_s] concatenated as 9 channels.
[ListBullet] Modify conv1 from 3 input channels to 12. Initialize channels 0:3 with official pretrained conv1 weights. Initialize each extra channel with mean(pretrained conv1 over RGB)/3 so the total initial response scale remains bounded.
[ListBullet] Spatial artifact code = layer3 output (256 channels, approximately 16×16 at 256 input). Global artifact code = L2-normalized GAP(layer4), 512-D.
[ListBullet] Attack-type auxiliary head: Linear(512, number_of_attack_macro_classes_seen_in_TRAIN), active only in GPAT-B1/B3.
[ListBullet] Identity adversary: GRL coefficient 1.0 followed by Linear(512, number_of_train_subjects), loss weight λ_idadv=0.1, active only in GPAT-B2/B3.
[Heading2] 9.3 Residual generator G_res — NAFResidualUNet
<TABLE>
| Stage | Resolution | Channels | Blocks / operation |
| Input | 128×128 | 12 | Concatenate target LL/LH/HL/HH (3 channels each); Conv3×3 → 32 |
| Enc1 | 128×128 | 32 | 2 NAFBlocks + FiLM(global z_a); downsample stride-2 |
| Enc2 | 64×64 | 64 | 2 NAFBlocks + FiLM; downsample |
| Enc3 | 32×32 | 128 | 4 NAFBlocks + FiLM; downsample |
| Enc4 | 16×16 | 256 | 8 NAFBlocks + FiLM; downsample |
| Bottleneck | 8×8 | 512 | Project spatial z_a 256→512, adaptive-pool to 8×8, concat with target stream, 1×1 fuse 1024→512, then 12 NAFBlocks |

| Dec3 | 32×32 | 128 | Upsample ×2 + skip Enc3 + 2 NAFBlocks + FiLM |

[ListBullet] HF delta activation = tanh; scale_hf = 0.15 in normalized image range [-1,1].
[ListBullet] LL delta activation = tanh; scale_ll = 0.05 before multiplication by γ. Since γ=0 in main run, ΔLL is ignored at reconstruction.
[ListBullet] Mask M = sigmoid(mask_logit). M is shared across RGB channels and resized only if a future wavelet level changes; no hard threshold during training.
[ListBullet] All upsampling = bilinear ×2 followed by Conv3×3; no transposed convolution.
[Heading2] 9.4 Artifact provenance map
A_rgb = mean_channel(|x_hat − x_t|)
A_freq = upsample(|ΔLH| + |ΔHL| + |ΔHH| + γ|ΔLL|)
A = Normalize(M ⊙ [0.5·A_rgb + 0.5·A_freq])
A is stored for every GPAT synthetic sample. It is NOT used by the common downstream detector in the main generator comparison. Artifact-map detector supervision is a separate GPAT-only extension experiment so that the generator comparison remains isolated.
[Heading1] 10. GPAT losses and exact training configuration
[Heading2] 10.1 Geometry losses
L_id = 1 − cos(F_id(x_t), F_id(x_hat))
L_lm = mean_k ||H_k(x_t) − H_k(x_hat)||_1
L_parse = 1 − Dice(P(x_t), P(x_hat)) + 0.1·KL(P(x_t) || P(x_hat))
L_low = ||LL(x_hat) − LL(x_t)||_1
[Heading2] 10.2 Artifact and regularization losses
L_artcon = 1 − cos(F_art(x_hat), F_art(x_s)) + max(0, cos(F_art(x_hat),F_art(x_t)) − cos(F_art(x_hat),F_art(x_s)) + 0.2)
L_spec = ||S_radial(x_hat) − S_radial(x_s)||_1 + 0.5·||S_orient(x_hat) − S_orient(x_s)||_1
L_budget = max(0,0.01−mean(A)) + max(0,mean(A)−0.25)
L_TV = TV(M) + 0.25·[TV(ΔLH)+TV(ΔHL)+TV(ΔHH)]
L_bg = ||(1−M_face_dilated)⊙(x_hat−x_t)||_1
F_art in the generator loss is a frozen ResNet-18 ImageNet feature extractor operating on the same fixed high-pass transform; it is not trained with attack labels and therefore does not leak Track-B metadata into GPAT-B0.

[ListBullet] Input = concatenate RGB image and fixed high-pass RGB → 6 channels, 256×256.

[Heading2] 10.6 Generator checkpoint selection
[ListBullet] Evaluate EMA checkpoint at every epoch 10–60 on VAL pairs fixed in val_pairs_v1.parquet.
[ListBullet] For each epoch compute ID cosine, NME, parsing Dice, low-frequency relative error and pairwise artifact similarity.
[ListBullet] Convert each metric to percentile rank across candidate epochs, oriented so higher is better.
[ListBullet] SelectionScore = 0.25·rank(ID) + 0.15·rank(Dice) + 0.25·rank(ArtSim) + 0.20·rank(−NME) + 0.15·rank(−LFErr).
[ListBullet] Choose maximum SelectionScore; tie-break by higher ArtSim, then earlier epoch. The TEST set is never involved.
[Heading1] 11. GPAT quality score and fairness isolation
Quality weighting is a secondary experiment, not a prerequisite for the main generator comparison. The RAW-MATCHED table uses GPAT outputs without semantic quality filtering except technical invalidity.
q_i = exp(Σ_k w_k · log(ε + g_tilde_k(i)))
[ListBullet] Technical invalidity hard reject: missing image, NaN/Inf, face detector failure, invalid dimensions, or image outside legal numeric range.
[ListBullet] QUALITY-MATCHED GPAT q uses normalized VAL-calibrated metrics: identity similarity, inverse NME, parsing Dice, inverse low-frequency error, artifact-source similarity and discriminator realism.
[ListBullet] Metric normalization bounds are 5th/95th percentiles measured on VAL generation only; clip normalized values to [0,1]. Equal weights w_k=1/6.
[ListBullet] No threshold is chosen on TEST. For quality filtering, accept top q-ranked samples until the common budget N_common is reached.
[Heading1] 12. Independent probes for generator evaluation
[Heading2] 12.1 ArtifactProbeNet
[ListBullet] Purpose: independent evaluation of whether synthetic samples preserve source attack-family evidence; it is never the GPAT E_art network.
[ListBullet] Backbone: torchvision ResNet-18 IMAGENET1K_V1.
[ListBullet] Input: 224×224 high-pass RGB using Gaussian residual k=9, σ=1.5.
[ListBullet] Labels: attack_macro over real TRAIN only. Using fine-grained labels in this evaluation probe is permitted even for Binary Track because the labels are not fed back to the generator or downstream detector.
[ListBullet] Loss: class-weighted cross-entropy with inverse-frequency weights clipped to [0.5, 3.0].
[ListBullet] Optimizer: AdamW lr=1e-4, weight_decay=1e-4; batch 64; 30 epochs; cosine decay; AMP; seeds [42] only because this is a frozen measurement tool.
[ListBullet] Checkpoint: maximum VAL macro-F1; freeze thereafter. Penultimate 512-D L2-normalized embedding is the artifact embedding.
[Heading2] 12.2 Fingerprint probe
[ListBullet] Extract 512-D high-pass features using the frozen ArtifactProbeNet backbone before its classifier.
[ListBullet] Train logistic regression C=1.0 to classify real spoof vs synthetic spoof. Balance classes 1:1.
[ListBullet] Use 5-fold GroupKFold with group = subject_id_global of the image’s visible face/target. Report mean ROC-AUC. AUC close to 0.5 is better; do not force it to exactly 0.5.
[ListBullet] Run separately per generator and per dataset to identify dataset-specific generator fingerprints.

[Heading1] 15. Validation threshold and test aggregation
[ListBullet] Frame probability = sigmoid(binary logit).
[ListBullet] Video probability = arithmetic mean of all sampled frame probabilities belonging to that canonical video.
[ListBullet] Primary reported metrics are video-level; frame-level metrics are secondary diagnostics.
[ListBullet] For each seed/method, compute threshold on VAL video scores by minimizing ACER. Sweep all unique score midpoints plus 0 and 1. Tie-break by threshold closest to 0.5.
[ListBullet] Freeze the selected threshold and apply it once to TEST. AUC and EER are threshold-free/derived and still use the same frozen TEST scores.
[Heading1] 16. Downstream metrics
APCER = # spoof videos predicted live / # spoof videos
BPCER = # live videos predicted spoof / # live videos
ACER = (APCER + BPCER) / 2
[ListBullet] Primary: ACER ↓, AUC ↑, EER ↓.
[ListBullet] Secondary: APCER ↓, BPCER ↓, accuracy, balanced accuracy, F1, TPR@FPR=1%, TPR@FPR=5%.
[ListBullet] Subgroups: dataset-wise ACER/AUC; attack_macro-wise APCER; SiW-Mv2 raw 14-type APCER when raw label coverage is valid.
[ListBullet] Report both frame-level and video-level tables, but video-level is the main paper result.

[Heading1] 18. Statistical analysis — frozen
[ListBullet] For every self-run downstream condition: seeds [42,1337,2026]; report mean ± sample standard deviation.
[ListBullet] Subject-level bootstrap 95% confidence interval: 10,000 bootstrap resamples of TEST subjects within each dataset, preserving all videos of a sampled subject. Compute CI for ACER and AUC.
[ListBullet] Primary paired comparison: GPAT-B0 vs each Binary-track baseline on identical TEST predictions. Use paired subject-level bootstrap for ΔACER and ΔAUC.
[ListBullet] Multiple-comparison correction: Holm–Bonferroni over the family of GPAT-B0 vs Binary-track baselines.
[ListBullet] Report effect size as absolute ΔACER percentage points and ΔAUC, not only p-values.
[ListBullet] A result is not described as “better” when the mean moves favorably but the CI/pairwise analysis is inconclusive; use “numerically lower/higher” instead.

[Heading1] 26. Unit tests and scientific sanity tests
[ListBullet] DWT→IDWT reconstruction error < 1e-5 in float32 on 100 random crops.
[ListBullet] GPAT γ=0 and all ΔHF=0 yields x_hat≈x_t (max absolute normalized error < 1e-5).
[ListBullet] GPAT artifact_scale=0 reduces output to target independent of spoof source.
[ListBullet] Pair manifest never pairs same subject; no pair references VAL/TEST for TRAIN synthesis.
[ListBullet] Split audit checks subject/video/sample intersections exactly empty.
[ListBullet] Downstream mixed sampler produces exactly 32 live / 16 real spoof / 16 synthetic per full batch; tail batches may be dropped.
[ListBullet] Threshold code reproduces ACER from confusion counts and is identical across methods.
[ListBullet] Video score aggregation uses only frames of that video and simple arithmetic mean.
[ListBullet] Plot scripts reject input tables that contain non-measured placeholders except in template mode.
[ListBullet] A full deterministic smoke test on a 2-subject-per-class toy subset must finish before full runs.
[Heading1] 27. Failure modes and mandatory handling
<TABLE>
| Failure | Required action |
| Missing subject IDs | BLOCK main split; do not silently use frame split |
| Third-party repo incompatible | isolated env/container; if still blocked, record dependency issue |
| Paper architecture underspecified | use explicit benchmark override only if defined; otherwise BLOCKED_BY_SOURCE_GAP |
| Generator produces too few valid samples | report failure count; do not duplicate images; QUALITY-MATCHED uses N_common |
| One attack type absent from TRAIN | do not invent examples; class absent from native classifier and report coverage |
| VAL class/attack absent | metric shown N/A; no resampling from TEST |
| GPU OOM | reduce physical batch and increase gradient accumulation while keeping effective batch fixed; log deviation as implementation-only |
| Method output different resolution | resize exactly once to 256 with canonical interpolation; log original resolution |
| One seed fails | rerun same seed after technical fix; never replace with a different seed |
| Unexpectedly high fingerprint AUC | do not hide; report and inspect difference maps/shortcuts |
</TABLE>

[Heading1] 29. Recommended paper narrative after experiments
[ListNumber] Problem: existing spoof-transfer approaches can alter facial content, directly copy frequency blocks, or generate the full image without an architectural preservation guarantee.
[ListNumber] Method: GPAT learns source-conditioned spoof evidence but restricts generation to bounded wavelet residuals; γ=0 hard-preserves target LL structure.
[ListNumber] Controlled benchmark: all methods are retrained on one pooled CASIA-FASD + MSU-MFSD + SiW-Mv2 subject-disjoint split and evaluated through the same downstream detector.
[ListNumber] Evidence 1: target preservation vs artifact transfer Pareto.
[ListNumber] Evidence 2: downstream benefit on real held-out test, not synthetic test.
[ListNumber] Evidence 3: Binary vs Native supervision distinguishes architectural benefit from metadata benefit.
[ListNumber] Evidence 4: ablations isolate hard wavelet lock, attack-type label, identity adversary, q and artifact-map extensions.
[ListNumber] Limitations: pooled held-out protocol measures within-pool subject/video generalization, not standard cross-dataset DG; conclusions must stay within this scope.

[Heading1] 31. Literature-derived supervision facts that must not be altered
[ListBullet] STDN explicitly positions its core spoof-trace learning as using only binary live-vs-spoof labels; spoof-type labels are not part of the main training supervision.
[ListBullet] DSDG explicitly uses a classifier on the spoof-pattern latent with y defined as the spoofing-type label.
[ListBullet] DiffFAS explicitly lists precise spoofing style label, texture preservation and identity consistency as critical properties; its training uses a Spoofing Style Pool and live-spoof pair images.
[ListBullet] PCGAN uses ground-truth live/attack labels to choose artifact conversion pairs; its PMN is a two-class live/attack detector.
[ListBullet] FAS-Aug creates task-specific artifacts from physical capture/recapture simulations rather than requiring a real spoof source for each synthetic sample.
[ListBullet] SiW-Mv2 has 14 named spoof types; the exact local availability must still be verified by inventory before Native/Full training.

[Heading1] Appendix C. Interpretation rules for common outcomes
<TABLE>
| Observed outcome | Permitted conclusion |
| GPAT preserves identity/geometry but ArtSim≈0 | GPAT is over-constrained; H1 alone is insufficient; artifact transfer not supported. |
| GPAT generator metrics are strong but downstream does not improve | GPAT produces controlled synthetic images but their utility for FAS training is not demonstrated. |
| Downstream improves but fingerprint AUC is very high | Improvement may rely on generator shortcut; contribution requires additional controls. |
| GPAT-B3 > B0 but B0 ≈ baselines | Metadata supervision contributes strongly; do not attribute all gain to architecture. |
| GPAT-B0 > binary baselines on both evaluators | Strong evidence that the transfer architecture itself contributes under equal label budget. |
| γ=0 has best geometry but γ>0 has clearly better downstream/ArtSim | Hard LL lock may be too restrictive; report Pareto and choose claim accordingly rather than forcing γ=0. |
| Only one seed is good | No robust improvement claim; report mean±std and investigate variance. |
</TABLE>
